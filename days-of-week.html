Cheat sheet
EnglishEspañolOrigin
  • Lowercase: Spanish does not capitalize the days of the week.
  • All masculine: el lunes, el sábado. "On Monday" is el lunes, with no preposition.
  • Plural: los lunes, los martes (the word does not change), but los sábados, los domingos.
